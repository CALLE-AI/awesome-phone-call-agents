# CallNeuron three-minute demo

This script keeps the story specific, shows real product depth, and avoids spending more than one CALL-E call.

## 0:00–0:25 — The problem

“Scholarship teams already know whom they want to contact. The risky work starts afterward: confirming the right adult, explaining approved terms consistently, avoiding sensitive voicemail, and turning a provider transcript into a human-owned follow-up. CallNeuron is the consent and disclosure layer around CALL-E.”

Show the operator guide and **Real calls placed: 0**.

## 0:25–1:10 — Local intake

1. Edit one offer sentence and point out the escalation boundary.
2. Download the CSV template or import a prepared XLSX/DOCX/PDF.
3. Show the required student code, employee code, masked phone, eligible record, and withdrawn-consent record.
4. Say: “The file never uploads. Derived rows remain on this device until Reset. Grades, finances, ID, and application evidence are excluded.”

## 1:10–1:50 — Consent and voicemail

1. Check the four operator attestations.
2. Show that the withdrawn record remains disabled.
3. Select one recipient.
4. Turn neutral voicemail on, reveal the exact wording, then turn it off for the live demonstration.
5. Point out the polite introduction, safe reason for calling, identity-first boundary, richer question flow, and exact human-handoff promise.

## 1:50–2:35 — CALL-E runtime

Use the deployed operator prototype and an entrant-owned, explicitly consented test phone only.

1. Connect the entrant’s CALL-E account.
2. Click **Create CALL-E plan · no call** and emphasize that the phone has not rung.
3. Show the immutable one-recipient summary, masked phone ending, English/Malaysia setting, voicemail state, and no-retry rule.
4. Open the exact instruction briefly.
5. Check “I understand the next button places one real phone call now,” then place the single approved call.
6. Show the recipient phone ringing or the short controlled conversation.

Never show access tokens, plan IDs, confirmation tokens, login secrets, or an unmasked personal number.

## 2:35–3:00 — Human outcome and impact

1. Show the provider status and transcript.
2. Assign **Interested** or the truthful staff disposition.
3. Export the privacy-minimal CSV.
4. Close with: “CALL-E performs the repetitive phone work. CallNeuron keeps selection, consent, sensitive disclosure, and final scholarship decisions with people.”

## Recording checklist

- Use fictional records except for the entrant-owned test number.
- Keep the video public on YouTube or Vimeo.
- Show the deployed operator-prototype URL and the GitHub pull-request URL.
- Keep the browser zoom at 100% and hide personal tabs, notifications, and account email.
- Record the real call once; do not retry automatically if it fails.
