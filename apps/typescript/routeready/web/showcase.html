The delivery route that calls ahead.

While the rider is still riding, RouteReady phones the next customers through CALL-E and re-orders the remaining stops around what they say.

  • 1One line, one call at a time: the soonest stop the rider reaches in 6 to 45 minutes.
  • 2An answer only counts when the customer's own words back it, so voicemail and receptionists change nothing.
  • 3"I need 15 minutes" moves that stop later, "not today" takes the trip off the route.

Open the rider app on a phone at /app. Press Start the day on either phone.
