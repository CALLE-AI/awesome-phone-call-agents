# Judging Criteria

## Real World Impact

Identify a real, specific phone-work problem and make a credible case that the solution benefits real users beyond the hackathon. A generic AI phone caller is not enough.

## Quality of the Idea

Present a creative, non-obvious use of CALL-E that is clear, appropriately scoped, and reusable by the community.

## Technical Implementation

Use CALL-E thoroughly and meaningfully in a working, non-trivial implementation. CALL-E must be imported and called at runtime.

## Product Experience and Demo

Provide a complete, coherent experience. The demonstration video should explain what the application does, who it helps, why phone calls are necessary, how CALL-E is used, and the result the agent returns.
