      05 What happens behind the screen

      DineLine finds the place. Agent Jake makes the call.

      The DineLine Concierge is the first CALL-E agent. It calls you, listens to the dinner you have in mind, and returns a structured request. n8n searches Google Places and gives you five choices. After you choose and approve one, Agent Jake is the second CALL-E agent: it calls the restaurant and returns a result that DineLine checks against the exact request you approved.
