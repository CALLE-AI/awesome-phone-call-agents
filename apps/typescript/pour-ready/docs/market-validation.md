# Market validation worksheet

Published requirements make the coordination problem credible, but they do not
prove that recipients will accept disclosed AI calls. Until interviews and an
opt-in test occur, describe PourReady as **evidence-backed but unvalidated**.

Useful background:

- [Australian training standard](https://training.gov.au/TrainingComponentFiles/CPC/CPCCCO4001_R2.pdf)
- [CCAA concrete pump delivery guidelines](https://www.ccaa.com.au/CCAA/CCAA/Docs/Industry/Concrete_Pump_Delivery_Guidelines.aspx)
- [Planning Ops scheduling alternative](https://planningops.com/)

## Who to interview

Recruit five people across AU or SG:

- concrete subcontractor or site supervisor;
- ready-mix dispatcher;
- pump operator or allocator;
- testing coordinator;
- general contractor operations lead.

Do not cold-call a public business. Recruit through a warm introduction, trade
group, existing contact, or written opt-in.

## Past-behavior questions

1. "Walk me through the last pour where one party was late or misunderstood the
   plan. What happened from booking to first truck?"
2. "Who called whom on the day before and morning of the pour?"
3. "What facts did you reconfirm verbally, and where were they recorded?"
4. "When did the last mismatch surface? What did waiting or rescheduling cost?"
5. "Show me the checklist, chat thread, or spreadsheet you used, if you can."
6. "Which calls would you never delegate to a disclosed automated assistant?"
7. "Would you accept a concise disclosed AI call that only repeats an existing
   plan? What would make you hang up?"
8. "Who would buy this, who would operate it, and who could block adoption?"
9. "Would you test this preview using controlled numbers for one fictional
   pour?"

Avoid "Would you use this?" as the primary evidence. Ask about the last real
event, artifacts, current spend, and concrete permission to test.

## Decision rule

Validation succeeds when at least three of five participants confirm manual
coordination calls from recent past behavior and at least one shares a real
checklist or tests the preview.

- **Pass:** describe the wedge and quote only consented, attributable evidence.
- **Fail:** say demand remains evidence-backed but unvalidated; pivot toward the
  role that demonstrated the strongest repeated pain.
- **Mixed:** research recipient acceptance and procurement constraints before
  adding product scope.

This repository contains no completed interviews and makes no traction claim.
