## Current-live items that must be re-verified

- Deadline and timezone conflict or amendment.
- Eligibility, team-size, geographic, age, and account rules.
- Current Devpost field labels, text limits, media formats, and public-access settings.
- Whether the overview's optional demo-URL wording has been reconciled with the rules' working-project-access requirement.
- Video hosting/access requirements and the hosted video's logged-out availability.
- Required integrations-list pull request timing and returned PR URL.
- Tie-breakers, prizes, and any rule amendments after 2026-08-19.
- Terms, privacy, intellectual-property, third-party service, and synthetic-data disclosures.

## Pre-submit stop gate

Do not submit until a human records the live source URL, access date/time, exact requirement text or a compliant short paraphrase, reviewer, and disposition for every item above. Confirm the working-project access plan, public video URL, awesome-list PR URL, and private CALL-E account email. Do not place credentials, phone values, raw transcripts, permits, provider IDs, or provider payloads in that record.
