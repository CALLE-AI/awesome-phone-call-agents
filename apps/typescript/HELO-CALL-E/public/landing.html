How it works

Three steps.
One phone call.

HELO doesn't send another text. It picks up the phone — and has a real conversation with your customer.

Booking created

Add a customer's name, phone number, and appointment time. Takes under ten seconds. HELO starts watching the clock.

CALL-E dials

An AI voice agent phones the customer, conducts a natural conversation, and works toward a confirmed outcome — no scripts, no hold music.

Outcome reported

Booked, unavailable, or uncertain — your dashboard updates the moment the call ends. If a retry is needed, it's scheduled automatically.

Why voice, not text

Text gets ignored.
A call gets answered.

Reminder texts are easy to dismiss. A real voice in real time is much harder to ignore — and much more likely to produce a decision.

📵

Texts go unread

Notification fatigue means your reminder gets buried. Voice cuts through because it demands a response in the moment.

🔄

Reschedules happen in conversation

A customer who can't make it is more likely to reschedule when talking to someone than when tapping a link no-one designed for them.

🏢

Works for any booking business

Restaurant reservation, medical appointment, salon slot, repair callout — HELO adapts its call goal to your business type.
