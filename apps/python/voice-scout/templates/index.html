AI phone qualification for small businesses. Configure the workflow for websites, IT, insurance, staffing, consulting, home services, or another business need.

See the demo

Voice Scout turns a repetitive first qualification call into a reviewable workflow. It identifies itself as automated, uses structured business context, and returns a result for human review.

Before a live call: use a CALL-E key and Goal ID that you own, enter a number you own or have permission to call, and confirm the call. Your key is used temporarily for this run and is not saved by this demo.
