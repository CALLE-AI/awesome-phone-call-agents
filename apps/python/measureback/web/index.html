    Language and privacy

    Choose an initial language. An adaptive preference asks the agent to follow the cook’s language when supported; this is best effort, not a guaranteed language detection feature. Keep the original quotations. Review any translation before sharing.

    This is a recipe capture tool, not food safety, nutrition or allergy advice. Do not use it for medical diets, canning or other safety critical instructions. No recording is published automatically.

    Preview does not dial. A separate approval is required. One call, no recurring schedule.

    Bring your own result

    Use a recipe JSON exported by MeasureBack. Import and validation run on your local server; the public example cannot receive your private file.
