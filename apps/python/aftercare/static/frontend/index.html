Project basis

Why post-discharge AI matters

Readmissions and delayed complication detection remain persistent gaps. AfterCare closes them with clinically grounded outreach — not another static reminder board.

Continuity breaks at discharge
Patients leave with instructions and red flags to watch — but most systems lack a reliable way to check in once they are home.
Early signals are easy to miss
Pain, non-adherence, fever, or breathing trouble often surface days later — when a timely call could have changed the path.
Manual outreach does not scale
Care teams cannot personally call every discharged patient. High-risk cases need priority without burning clinician time.

What we built — a modular follow-up agent that schedules calls, converses through CALL-E, receives transcripts, scores risk, escalates emergencies, and presents patient status in one operational workspace.

How it works

One clinical loop. Clear ownership.

From discharge record to clinician action — auditable, consent-aware, and designed for working care teams.

01

Register the patient

Capture discharge context, clinician ownership, and consent. Live calls never proceed without consent on file.

02

Schedule outreach

Queue follow-ups for a precise time or trigger immediately. Missed calls retry within clear attempt limits.

03

CALL-E converses

A goal-driven voice agent checks symptoms, medication adherence, and recovery concerns — not a rigid script.

04

Score and escalate

Transcripts become structured findings and risk levels. Critical signals alert the care team right away.

Services

What AfterCare offers

Practical capabilities for hospitals that need reliable post-discharge outreach without expanding call-center headcount.

Automated voice follow-up
Outbound clinical check-ins through CALL-E, with dry-run mode and consent gates for live outreach.
Symptom & risk intelligence
Extract symptoms, severity, and compliance. Score risk as low, medium, high, or critical.
Emergency escalation
Keyword backstops and risk thresholds notify clinicians when urgent concerns appear.
Disease-aware protocols
Structure conversations around discharge diagnosis so outreach stays clinically relevant.
Patient registry & status
A living roster with consent state, current risk, and the latest call outcome.
Transcripts & history
Review summaries, full transcripts, and symptom findings from every completed call.
