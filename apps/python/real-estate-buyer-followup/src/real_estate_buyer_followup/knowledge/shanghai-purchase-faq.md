## [kb_sh_eligibility] Purchase eligibility: residency, contributions and residence permits
- published: 2026-02-25
- effective: 2026-02-26
- reviewed: 2026-11-02
- status: verified_summary
- source: https://fgj.sh.gov.cn/gzdt/20260225/02afee226c90485ca5183cd213e22815.html

The cited February 2026 Shanghai housing authority explanation describes these routes for non-Shanghai registered households or adult single buyers: one year of continuous Shanghai social-insurance or individual income-tax contributions allows unrestricted purchases outside the Outer Ring and one home inside; three continuous years allows two inside. A Shanghai residence permit held for five years permits one home citywide under the document's conditions without contribution/tax evidence.

Applicability: establish registration, existing homes, continuous contribution/tax years, residence-permit history and property location. Being in Shanghai or having a budget does not establish eligibility. Residence-permit points are not a universal threshold. The broker must verify actual eligibility with the transaction center.

## [kb_sh_newhome_points] New-home points: subscription ranking is not general eligibility
- published: 2026-09-01
- effective: 2026-09-01
- reviewed: 2026-11-02
- status: reference_only
- source: https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col5943/20260908/1317769.html

A September 2026 report republished on the Changning government portal describes a Shanghai new-home project using points to shortlist lottery participants when subscriptions were high. General purchase eligibility and project-specific subscription ranking are different stages; there is no single score required for every purchase.

Applicability: this entry supplies mechanism background, not a universal points cutoff, fixed shortlist ratio or current project threshold. Clarify whether the customer means eligibility, residence-permit points or new-home subscription ranking, then establish new versus resale and the specific project. Do not apply new-project subscription processes to resale homes. A and B have no confirmed new/resale status in the fixture; do not invent their points rules. Ask the broker to check the project's subscription notice.

## [kb_sh_commercial_mortgage] Commercial mortgages: minimum down payment is not personal approval
- published: 2026-08-20
- effective: 2026-08-21
- reviewed: 2026-11-02
- status: verified_summary
- source: https://fgj.sh.gov.cn/zcjd/20260820/90818bbaa9c646e49f42ac760e1882ac.html

The cited August 2026 housing authority explanation gives a minimum commercial-mortgage down payment of 15% for a first home; for a second home, 25% inside the Outer Ring and 15% outside, including all of Baoshan and Jiading. These are policy minimums in that document, not a bank's promise to an individual.

Applicability: establish the bank's first/second-home classification, location and loan type. A CNY 5m budget does not determine down payment, loan amount, monthly payment or approval. The bank confirms rates, income/credit checks, term and final loan ratio under application-time rules. B costs CNY 5.3m and exceeds the customer's CNY 5m ceiling; do not raise her budget by suggesting more borrowing.

## [kb_sh_provident_fund] Provident fund: conditional maximums only
- published: 2026-02-25
- effective: 2026-02-26
- reviewed: 2026-11-02
- status: verified_summary
- source: https://fgj.sh.gov.cn/gzdt/20260225/02afee226c90485ca5183cd213e22815.html

The same February 2026 explanation raises the maximum first-home household provident-fund loan to CNY 2.4m. Subject to qualifying multiple-child and green-building increases, the cited household ceiling can reach CNY 3.24m. Households that used provident-fund lending before may reapply under conditions including no Shanghai home, or only one with the previous provident-fund loan repaid.

Applicability: a household ceiling is neither an individual ceiling nor Ms. Zhang's entitlement. Contributions, household circumstances, existing homes, repayment and property conditions are unknown. Record them for verification; do not promise the maximum loan, payment or rate. The provident-fund center determines the actual entitlement.

## [kb_sh_replacement_subsidy] Replacement purchase: temporary subsidy requires verification
- published: 2026-08-20
- effective: 2026-08-21
- reviewed: 2026-11-02
- status: verified_summary
- source: https://fgj.sh.gov.cn/zcjd/20260820/90818bbaa9c646e49f42ac760e1882ac.html

The cited August 2026 document describes temporary replacement arrangements: between August 21, 2026 and March 31, 2027, a qualifying newly built commercial home outside the Outer Ring must be registered online, with a Shanghai resale home sold within one year before or after registration. The loan subsidy is 1% of the new-home loan, capped at CNY 50,000 per home and subject to funds remaining. Under additional conditions including sale of a resale home inside the Outer Ring, combined support may reach CNY 80,000.

Applicability: verify old-home sale, new-home type/location, registration dates and funding availability. An upgrade-buyer label alone is insufficient. Do not subtract an assumed subsidy from the price or budget or promise approval. After expiration this entry is historical reference only.
