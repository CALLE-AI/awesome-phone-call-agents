# Viewing Feedback Analysis and Follow-up Guide

Broker-maintained business experience for communication planning. These are conditional strategies, not facts the customer has already expressed or legal policy.

## [kb_guidance_price] Customer considers a property too expensive
- published: 2026-11-02
- effective: 2026-11-02
- reviewed: 2026-11-02
- status: broker_guidance
- source: broker://knowledge/viewing-followup

Scenario: the customer considers a property too expensive.

Business guidance:
- First establish whether the customer values the property's other features.
- Distinguish poor value for money from an absolute inability to raise the budget.
- If the customer is open to negotiation, record conditions without promising an outcome.
- If the customer explicitly rejects over-budget homes, respect the budget constraint in later recommendations.

## [kb_guidance_commute] Customer may accept a longer metro walk
- published: 2026-11-02
- effective: 2026-11-02
- reviewed: 2026-11-02
- status: broker_guidance
- source: broker://knowledge/viewing-followup

Scenario: the customer says a longer metro walk may be acceptable.

Business guidance:
- This may relax a preference; it does not mean transport no longer matters.
- Use actual viewing feedback to clarify acceptable commute trade-offs.
- Without a confirmed distance, do not overwrite the original precise value.
