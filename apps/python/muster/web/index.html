The protocol

One call, four stages, in this order. The model extracts what was heard. Code alone decides what it was worth.

  1. I

    Reach

    Classify what answered the line before anything else is asked: the subject, a third party, voicemail, an automated menu, or no answer at all. Silence is recorded as silence. It is never read as a result in either direction.

  2. II

    Self-identification under disclosure

    State plainly who is calling, why, and what the call will cause, then ask the person to confirm that they are the subject. The disclosure is what turns a shrug down the line into a statement somebody made about themselves.

  3. III

    Freshness challenge

    A nonce minted for this call alone: three words to repeat in order, and today's day of the week. A recording made yesterday cannot satisfy a question minted seconds ago, however faithfully it carries the subject's own voice.

  4. IV

    Knowledge challenge

    Two prompts drawn from a set enrolled out of band by a person. A prompt is marked co-resident safe when somebody sharing the house would not plausibly know the answer. Only a co-resident safe prompt can carry a confirmation on its own.

Honest limits

Three things this register cannot do. They are written here rather than discovered later.

  • No voice biometrics. CALL-E returns transcripts, not audio. There is no speaker verification to be had from text, so a synthesised voice reading the right answers would pass every stage of this protocol.
  • A well-briefed relative passes. The knowledge challenge defeats a stranger holding a reassigned number. It does not defeat somebody who has lived alongside the subject for forty years and knows the enrolled prompts.
  • Muster can confirm life. It can never confirm death. No grade in this register concludes that anybody has died. Contra means a person must look at this, and nothing more. Concluding a death from a telephone call is precisely how living pensioners are wrongly cut off.

Only confirmed live closes without a person. Every other grade opens a human case. This never stops a payment.
