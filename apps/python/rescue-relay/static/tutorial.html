Follow the rescue

TRY IT YOURSELF

One rescue. Every step.

Open the app and choose Try the demo. Follow the steps below at your own pace.

01

Keep your circle ready

In Trusted contacts, add the people and organisations you rely on. Save their services and permission to receive rescue calls. Demo contacts are already included.

02

Describe what you see

Tell us what happened, where the animal is and your budget. The example starts with a dog that cannot put weight on one leg, on Willow Road.

03

Clarify the outcome

Choose Send. Describe what success means: trained responders, safe transport and a receiving veterinarian. Select the proposed goal and confirm it to save. Then choose Find help separately.

04

Find available help

After saving the goal, choose Find help to begin inquiries. The first complete offer appears with its included tasks, quoted price and timing. Greenway Rescue quotes USD 300 against a USD 200 budget.

05

Compare, then choose

Choose Find another option, then compare complete plans. Kindred Rescue quotes USD 150. Select that plan. Details & history keeps the saved replies within reach.

06

Approve your helper

Choose Review & approve. Check the helper, tasks and price limits, then tick the approval box. Only the selected helper receives the confirmation callback.

07

Keep the rescue current

Record On the way, Arrived and Finished only as each update is confirmed. A helper’s agreement is not an automatic arrival or a finished rescue.

08

Close the loop

When the work is finished, add where the animal is now and confirm it is safe. Copy a shareable update. Your closing note, progress and replies stay with the saved report.

When the plan needs another step

A helper still needs confirmation

Use the condition-check action to ask about the unresolved prerequisite. In the demo, choose the response you want to test. A pending answer stays pending; it does not approve work.

The final reply or price is incomplete

Review the saved reply. Use Confirm with helper when offered to reconfirm that helper’s tasks and approved price. A changed price, refusal or unresolved provider status remains visible rather than being treated as success.

You need to stop or return later

Use Stop during an inquiry to stop subsequent work. A call already submitted to the provider may continue. Use Switch report to return to saved cases. Recording progress and closing a rescue always require your confirmation.

    RUN THE APP LOCALLY

    Your workspace, ready.

    From the rescue-relay-app folder, install the Python dependencies and start the server. Use a virtual environment as described in README.md. The default is Demo mode.

    python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python run.py

# Open http://127.0.0.1:8000

    This walkthrough is open from a file. Start the app, then open the local address above in your browser.
