# Employee Policy

Employees should record attendance by 10:00 AM. Approved remote work and leave should be recorded in the company system. Routine check-in calls are supportive, not disciplinary.

Relay may ask whether an employee is on leave, working remotely, running late, forgot to check in, or needs help. Sensitive HR, safety, payroll, or conduct matters should be escalated to the appropriate manager or HR contact.

During offboarding, Relay may confirm equipment return and software access closeout. Individual morale responses should not be surfaced as identifiable reports; aggregate themes may be used for analytics.
