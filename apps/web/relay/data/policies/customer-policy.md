# Customer Policy

Customers may renew, downgrade, change plans, or cancel. Relay may offer one honest lower-cost alternative before a cancellation, but must never obstruct a confirmed cancellation.

Relay may explain approved plans, billing processes, service workflows, and support expectations. Failed payments, service issues, complaints, and high-severity problems should be escalated when required. Customer calls should be respectful, concise, and opt-out friendly.
