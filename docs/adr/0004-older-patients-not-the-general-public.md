# Older patients, not the general public

The product targets patients who find the telephone hardest to use. This overrules
the 19 August 2026 team minute, which recorded a move away from an elderly focus
toward "an AI-driven appointment booking system for general clinic use".

The minute is recorded as a scope decision, but it removes the premise. Queue
Absorption and Carried Words are only defensible because the patient cannot
reasonably do it themselves; for a patient who can use the online portal, "use the
portal" is a complete answer and the workflow has no reason to exist. The narrower
audience also costs nothing to build — the same code, a slower script, and a
different demo.

Recorded here so the decision is not silently re-litigated against the minute.
