# The Rebooking Call speaks from a closed list, and the date of birth waits to be asked

The PRD holds two rules that are not the same rule. Its Read Scope table grants the
Rebooking Call a surname and a date of birth with the words "yes — reception asks". Its
script hands both over in the opening sentence, unprompted: *"Margaret Wilson, date of
birth 4 March 1943."* Giving an identifier when asked and broadcasting it to whoever
picked up are different acts, and only one of them is what the table describes.

The agent may speak from three sources and no others: the Practice's own name, the
contents of the Release — the Booking Envelope and the approved Carried Words — and the
Patient's identifiers. This is a whitelist, built the way the Never-Ask Rule is built: we
name what may be said rather than trying to enumerate everything that may not.

Within that third source, the name goes out in the opening and the date of birth waits to
be asked. Reception cannot open a record without it, so she will ask within a turn, and
the cost of waiting is that one turn. What the turn buys is that a call which reached a
wrong number, an old number, or a handset on speakerphone in a waiting room has disclosed
a name and nothing more.

Anything outside the three sources is answered with one fixed sentence that is never
improvised, and then the call returns to the business of scheduling. This is the Safety
Line's shape borrowed for the second call: a pattern this team already trusts because it
removes a decision from the model rather than asking the model to make it well. Note that
after ADR 0009 the approved words may legitimately be empty, so "I'm only able to pass on
what she said" is sometimes the whole of what the agent has — and that is a complete
answer, not a failure.

## Consequences

The Rebooking Call runs a turn or two longer than the script in the PRD, and the PRD's
opening line changes. Letting the model field reception's questions naturally would read
better on the phone and is refused for the reason ADR 0007 gives: a scan after the call
can find a leak but cannot unsay it. Speech is the one output in this system with no
second reading available, so the constraint has to sit in front of it.
