# Research Papers & Official Documentation

These are research inspirations, not evidence that this local mock scorer implements RLHF or a validated LLM judge.

1. **Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior** (UIST 2023)
   - **URL/DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1145/3586183.3606763
   - **Relevance**: Core architectural design for the "Memory Stream" and "Reflection" components that allow the agent to learn from past interactions.

2. **Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena** (NeurIPS 2023)
   - **URL/arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685
   - **Relevance**: Studies LLM judging and its limitations; it does not establish objective judgments or validate this lexical mock scorer.

3. **Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback** (NeurIPS 2023)
   - **URL/arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651
   - **Relevance**: Demonstrates that LLMs can generate actionable self-feedback to improve their own future generations.

4. **NIST AI RMF 1.0** (NIST, 2023)
   - **URL**: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf
   - **Relevance**: Outlines standard governance for systems that learn from continuous feedback loops.
