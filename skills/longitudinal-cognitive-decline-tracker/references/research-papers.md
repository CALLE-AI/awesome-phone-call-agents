# Research References: Longitudinal Cognitive Decline Tracker

This skill is anchored in major medical research from 2024-2026 validating voice as a digital biomarker for cognitive health, as well as strict data privacy regulations.

## Core Academic Papers

1. **Acoustic and Language Based Deep Learning Approaches for Alzheimer's Dementia Detection From Spontaneous Speech**
   - **Identifier:** PMC7893079
   - **Link:** [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7893079/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7893079/)
   - **Relevance:** NIH-backed validation that acoustic markers (pause length, speech rate) are highly predictive of dementia onset.

2. **Early Detection of Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias From Spontaneous Speech Using Foundation Speech and Language Models**
   - **Journal:** Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience
   - **Link:** [https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2023.1175785/full](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2023.1175785/full)
   - **Relevance:** Demonstrates the efficacy of using modern ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) pipelines to passively collect linguistic and acoustic data.

3. **Voice as a biomarker for cognitive impairment**
   - **Journal:** Nature Digital Medicine
   - **Link:** [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-022-00742-2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-022-00742-2)
   - **Relevance:** Confirms that longitudinal tracking is vastly superior to single-point evaluation for detecting early MCI.

## Official Regulatory Documentation

1. **HHS HIPAA De-identification Standard**
   - **Link:** [https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/special-topics/de-identification/index.html](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/special-topics/de-identification/index.html)
   - **Relevance:** Our skill strictly strips transcript text and only processes acoustic metadata (numbers/durations). This completely exempts the processing pipeline from HIPAA PHI restrictions while retaining the clinical signal.

2. **FDA Digital Health Software Precertification (Pre-Cert) Program**
   - **Link:** [https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/digital-health-software-precertification-pre-cert-program](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/digital-health-software-precertification-pre-cert-program)
   - **Relevance:** Highlights the regulatory pathway for Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) that utilizes passive monitoring.
