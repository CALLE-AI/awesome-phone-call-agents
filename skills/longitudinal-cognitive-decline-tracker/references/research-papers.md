# Research Scope — Longitudinal Cognitive Decline Tracker

This offline prototype demonstrates a linear trend in supplied pause durations.
It does not implement or validate a clinical speech-biomarker model. Its 50ms
threshold and legacy clinical field names are not evidence of diagnostic accuracy.
No sensitivity, dementia-detection, or regulatory-readiness claim is made.

The original Nature paper attribution was unrelated to the claimed voice
biomarker result and has been removed. Other paper-to-implementation mappings
are not independently established and are not relied on as clinical validation.

Removing transcript text does not automatically de-identify acoustic metadata
or exempt processing from privacy obligations. No HIPAA exemption or FDA approval
is claimed. Use fictional inputs only; do not make real clinical decisions from
this reference.
