# Research Papers & Official Documentation

Background reading only; these publications do not validate this fixed pause-ratio threshold or establish clinical performance.

1. **Detection of Mild Dyspnea from Pairs of Speech Recordings** (IEEE ICASSP, 2020)
   - **URL/DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1109/icassp40776.2020.9054751
   - **Relevance**: Provides the acoustic feature extraction models for identifying respiratory variations and abnormal pause mechanics.

2. **Biomarkers in respiratory diseases** (Breathe editorial, 2019)
   - **URL/DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1183/20734735.0329-2019
   - **Relevance**: General respiratory-biomarker editorial; it does not validate an acoustic pause ratio as a respiratory-failure detector.

3. **COVID-19-related voice disorders: a scoping review** (PubMed, 2026)
   - **URL/PMID**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42138851/
   - **Relevance**: Modern acoustic analysis of breathing alterations caused by severe viral respiratory infections.

4. **Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)** (FDA, 2023)
   - **URL**: https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/software-medical-device-samd
   - **Relevance**: Regulatory framework for AI algorithms evaluating biological states.
