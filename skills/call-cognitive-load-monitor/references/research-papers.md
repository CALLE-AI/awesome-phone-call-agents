# Research Scope — call-cognitive-load-monitor

This is an offline lexical heuristic, not a validated cognitive measurement.
The supplied weights, phase labels and consent flags are demonstration choices;
they do not reproduce a published acoustic model, administer NASA-TLX, or prove
that a caller understood or validly consented. No scientific performance result
or regulatory compliance determination is claimed for this implementation.

The original paper-to-implementation mappings and precise legal clause summaries
have not been independently established and must not be used as validation.
Performance numbers in the skill are unvalidated design targets.

For general regulatory background, consult the [FCA Consumer Duty policy](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/policy/ps22-9.pdf)
and the [official EU AI Act text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689).
These sources do not certify this heuristic or turn its flags into legal findings.
A human must review any proposed follow-up; the script does not send messages,
schedule calls, or decide consent validity.
