# Research Foundation - call-guess-forcing-auditor

Each citation below is mapped to the concrete design decisions it drives.
Citations are motivation and anchoring only: this skill implements
heuristic lexicons, honestly labeled as heuristics, and none of the
papers' models, benchmarks, or performance results are reproduced or
validated here.

| Citation | Design decisions it drives |
|---|---|
| Method anchor: Gudjonsson, G. H., "A new scale of interrogative suggestibility", Personality and Individual Differences, 1984. <https://doi.org/10.1016/0191-8869(84)90069-2> | The Yield construct (uncertain answers accepted after pressure) and Shift construct (answers changed after negative feedback) define the two auditable events. |
| Instrument validation: Hein, M. et al., "Psychometric Properties of a German Online Version of the Gudjonsson Suggestibility Scale 1", Frontiers in Psychology 12:718805, 2021. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.718805> | Interrogative suggestibility is a measurable, stable individual difference - the behavior is auditable, not vibes. |
| Failure-mode evidence: Chan, S., Pataranutaporn, P., Suri, A., Zulfikar, W., Maes, P., Loftus, E. F., "Conversational AI Powered by Large Language Models Amplifies False Memories in Witness Interviews", arXiv 2408.04681, 2024. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04681> | An LLM interviewer induced over 3x more false memories than control (N=200), with misplaced confidence persisting a week - the failure mode is real in deployed conversational AI. |
| Normative: UNODC, "Investigative Interviewing for Criminal Investigation" (names the PEACE model). <https://www.unodc.org/unodc/en/justice-and-prison-reform/investigative-interviewing.html> | International guidance prescribes open, non-coercive questioning - the craft protocol transplants that standard to outbound calls. |

## Verification notes and corrections

- All URLs were source-page verified on 2026-10-19.
- CRITICAL correction: the false-memories paper is arXiv 2408.04681
  (2024), NOT 2503.17473 - the latter is an unrelated Fang et al.
  longitudinal chatbot study that a Google Scholar snippet misattributes
  to this topic.
- Hein et al. (2021) validates GSS Scale 1 (not GSS 2).
- The method anchor is the 1984 founding paper (Crossref DOI pinned),
  not the 1987 parallel-form note.
- Chan et al. is an arXiv preprint - no peer-reviewed venue is claimed
  for it.
- The lexicon heuristics in this skill are NOT validated instruments.
  They are demonstration-grade pattern lists; the cited papers' psychometric
  results do not transfer to them.
