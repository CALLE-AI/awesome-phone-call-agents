# Research Scope — call-script-compliance-auditor

The rule library is an experimental phrase checklist for human review. It is not
a complete transcription of any law, a validated compliance model, or permission
to dial. PASS means only that the selected heuristic checks passed.

No 95% or 97.9% precision result is established for this implementation. The
original research summaries and exact clause-to-rule mappings are not relied on
as evidence of legal correctness. Qualified review is needed before real use.

## General background

- [FCA Consumer Duty policy](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/policy/ps22-9.pdf)

- [HHS HIPAA privacy guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/laws-regulations/index.html)

These sources provide background only. The demonstration labels such as US_TCPA
and US_HIPAA do not establish that every legal requirement or exception is covered.
