# Research Papers & Official Documentation

## Core Anti-Spoofing & Deepfake Detection Research

1. **ASVspoof 2021: Towards Spoofed and Deepfake Speech Detection in the Wild**
   - **Publication**: IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing (TASLP), 2023
   - **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1109/TASLP.2023.3285283
   - **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02437
   - **Relevance**: The gold-standard benchmark challenge for evaluating speech liveness detectors against both text-to-speech (TTS) and voice-conversion (VC) attacks. Defines the evaluation metrics (Equal Error Rate - EER) and countermeasure baselines used by this skill.

2. **ADD 2022: The First Audio Deep Synthesis Detection Challenge**
   - **Publication**: ICASSP 2022 — IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing
   - **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP43922.2022.9746939
   - **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08433
   - **Relevance**: Introduces the critical **Partially Fake Audio Detection** (PF) track, addressing real-world attacks where only fragments of a call are synthetically replaced — a key threat model for phone-call agent environments.

3. **FTC Voice Cloning Challenge — Winners Announced April 2024**
   - **Publication**: U.S. Federal Trade Commission Official Press Release, April 2024
   - **URL**: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-announces-winners-voice-cloning-challenge
   - **Relevance**: Background on voice-cloning risks and distinct mitigation approaches. DeFake uses adversarial distortion to resist cloning, not watermarking; these projects do not validate this local scorer.

4. **NIST Speaker Recognition Evaluation (SRE) Program**
   - **URL**: https://sre.nist.gov
   - **Relevance**: Background on speaker-recognition evaluation. This contribution does not establish validation against NIST corpora.

## Supporting Literature

5. **ASVspoof 2021: Accelerating Progress in Spoofed and Deepfake Speech Detection** (Overview Paper)
   - **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2109.00537
   - **Relevance**: Provides the taxonomy of attack types (A01–A19 TTS systems, V01–V06 VC systems) that defines the threat model for this skill's feature extraction design.
