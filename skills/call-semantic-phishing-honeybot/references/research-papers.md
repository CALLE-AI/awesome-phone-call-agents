# Research Papers & Official Documentation

Research inspiration only; these sources do not validate the local regex scorer or any phone-call effectiveness claims.

1. **Automatic Scam-Baiting Using ChatGPT** (IEEE TrustCom, 2023)
   - **URL/DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1109/trustcom60117.2023.00264
   - **Relevance**: Studies email scam-baiting with ChatGPT, not validation of this phone-workflow helper.

2. **Fraudsters target the elderly: Behavioural evidence from randomised controlled scam-baiting experiments** (Security Journal, 2024)
   - **URL/DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1057/s41284-023-00410-4
   - **Relevance**: Evaluates email scam-baiting behavior; it does not establish voice-call efficacy.

3. **AI-in-the-Loop: Privacy Preserving Real-Time Scam Detection and Conversational Scam-baiting** (POPETs, 2024/2026)
   - **URL/DOI**: https://doi.org/10.56553/popets-2026-0006
   - **Relevance**: Proposes a privacy-preserving architecture where the AI intercepts calls and baits scammers without leaking user data.

4. **MITRE ATT&CK - Social Engineering Techniques** (MITRE, 2024)
   - **URL**: https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1566/
   - **Relevance**: Defines the standardized taxonomy (T1566) for classifying the threat intelligence extracted by the honeybot.
