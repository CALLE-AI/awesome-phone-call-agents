# Research Scope — call-negotiation-coach

This offline demonstration uses familiar negotiation concepts such as BATNA,
interest exploration and a two-axis concern model to organize suggested tactics.
It does not implement a published reinforcement-learning strategy, enforce a
reservation price, accept a deal, or execute a financial transaction.

The original arXiv:2411.05816 attribution was unrelated and has been removed.
Other supplied empirical mappings, percentage improvements and institutional
study attributions are not independently verified and are not claimed as
validation of this implementation. Detection targets are unvalidated aspirations.

The percentage matcher can flag numbers spoken by either party without proving
they were agreed terms. Treat all strategy cards and debriefs as suggestions for
human review, not as outcome guarantees or an autonomous acceptance policy.
