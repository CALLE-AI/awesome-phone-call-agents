# Research Papers & Official Documentation

## Core Entrainment Theory & Measurement

1. **Communication Accommodation Theory: Past Accomplishments, Current Trends, and Future Prospects**
   - **Authors**: Howard Giles, America L. Edwards, Joseph B. Walther
   - **Publication**: *Language Sciences*, Elsevier, 2023
   - **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.langsci.2023.101571
   - **Relevance**: The definitive 2023 update to CAT from the theory's originator. Formalizes the convergence/divergence mechanism that this skill implements computationally, including updated principles for mediated (telephone/digital) communication contexts.

2. **Modeling Vocal Entrainment in Conversational Speech using Deep Unsupervised Learning**
   - **Authors**: Md Nasir, Brian Baucom, Craig Bryan, Shrikanth Narayanan, Panayiotis Georgiou
   - **Publication**: *IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing*, Vol. 13, No. 3, July 2022
   - **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1109/TAFFC.2020.3024972
   - **Relevance**: Provides the deep unsupervised learning framework for computing entrainment distance in the feature space. The cosine similarity aggregation approach in this skill is directly adapted from the triplet-network methodology proposed in this paper.

3. **A Context-Aware Computational Approach for Measuring Vocal Entrainment in Dyadic Conversations**
   - **Authors**: Rimita Lahiri, Md Nasir, Catherine Lord, So Hyun Kim, Shrikanth Narayanan
   - **Publication**: arXiv preprint, 2022
   - **DOI/arXiv**: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.03279
   - **Relevance**: Introduces cross-subject attention mechanisms to model intra- and interpersonal prosodic signals simultaneously — the mathematical basis for the context-aware feature comparison used in Step 2 of this skill's pipeline.

## Regulatory & Standards Documentation

4. **ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Artificial Intelligence Management System**
   - **URL**: https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html
   - **Relevance**: Governs the responsible deployment of AI systems that autonomously adapt their behavior in real-time (as this skill does). Specifically, the transparency and explainability requirements (Section 6.1) apply to the TTS directive system, which must log every adjustment for audit purposes.

## Supporting Literature

5. **Prosody and Rapport in Human-Agent Interaction: A Survey**
   - **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01775
   - **Relevance**: Survey of prosodic accommodation techniques in conversational agents, documenting empirical improvements in user satisfaction of 12–23% from pitch and rate alignment.

6. **Towards an Unsupervised Entrainment Distance in Conversational Speech using Deep Neural Networks**
   - **Publication**: Interspeech 2018
   - **URL**: https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2018/nasir18_interspeech.html
   - **Relevance**: Foundational predecessor to reference [2] above. Establishes the baseline unsupervised entrainment distance computation that the current skill's architecture evolved from.
