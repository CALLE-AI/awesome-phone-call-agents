# Research & Regulatory Scope - call-cancellation-respect-auditor

The verdict lexicons are an experimental heuristic checklist for human
review. They are not a complete transcription of any law, a validated
enforcement model, or permission to act on a real account. A verdict such as
RETENTION_DARK_PATTERN means only that the selected heuristic patterns
matched; qualified human review is required before any real use.

## Foundation

1. **Method / taxonomy**: Nembaware, F. E., & Sousa, S. (2025). "Dark
   patterns in subscription service cancellation processes." Proceedings of
   the 36th Annual Conference of the European Association of Cognitive
   Ergonomics (ECCE 2025), Tallinn, Estonia. ACM. doi:10.1145/3746175.3746211,
   pp. 1-4. <https://doi.org/10.1145/3746175.3746211>
   - Drives: the obstacle / insistence / guilt detection families here are
     mapped from the paper's obstruction, forced-action, and urgency
     taxonomy families.
   - Honest scope note: visual-only categories in the taxonomy (sneaking
     interface, visual scarcity) have no phone-transcript analogue and are
     out of scope for this skill.
   - CORRECTION NOTE: some secondary sources cite this paper as 10 pages;
     Crossref and the ACM DL record pp. 1-4.

2. **Statutory**: Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act (ROSA), Pub. L.
   111-345, 15 U.S.C. Sec. 8403 "Negative option marketing on the Internet".
   Sec. 8403(a)(3) requires sellers to "provide[] simple mechanisms for a
   consumer to stop recurring charges".
   <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/8403>
   - Drives: the obstacle family (channel redirection, in-person-only,
     approval loops, delayed effect) implements "as easy to cancel as to
     sign up" in transcript terms.

3. **In-force regulation**: New York City DCWP "Click-to-Cancel" rule -
   announced 2026-04-09 (nyc.gov: "Mayor Mamdani, Commissioner Levine
   Announce First-In-The-Nation Municipal 'Click to Cancel' Rule to End
   Subscription Traps"), adopted 2026-07-10, effective 2026-10-01, fines
   from $525 per violation - the first municipal rule of its kind in the US.
   - Drives: why the skill exists for phone-channel enforcement auditing -
     municipal rules now reach how cancellations are handled, and phone
     transcripts are the conduct record.

4. **Honest legal-state erratum**: The FTC amended Negative Option Rule
   ("Click-to-Cancel", 2024) was VACATED by the U.S. Court of Appeals for
   the Eighth Circuit on 2025-07-08 (Nos. 24-3137 lead). This skill
   therefore anchors on ROSA (the statute, still in force) and the NYC
   rule, NOT the vacated federal rule. Recorded here to avoid stale-law
   claims.

These sources provide background only. Matching a heuristic pattern does
not establish that any statute, rule, or regulation was violated.
