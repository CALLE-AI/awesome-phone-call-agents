# Reservation Confirmation Policy

## Protocol

1. Re-contact the selected hotel's reservations desk after the traveler approves the offer and authorizes payment.
2. Confirm the agreed stay dates, guest party size, room type, negotiated price, and inclusions.
3. Obtain the hotel's reservation/confirmation number.
4. Record any special check-in notes or instructions provided by the staff.
5. If the hotel cannot confirm the reservation or retracts the previous quote, flag the status as `failed` immediately.
