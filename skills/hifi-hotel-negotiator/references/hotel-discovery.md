# Hotel Discovery & Stay Verification Reference

## Objective
Establish phone contact with the hotel front desk or reservation department to verify actual room availability and all associated financial conditions for the requested stay.

## Inquiry Order

1. **Greeting & Identification**:
   - "Hello, I am calling on behalf of a guest planning a stay at your property."
2. **Date & Occupancy Verification**:
   - "Could you check availability for [check_in] to [check_out] for [adults] adults in [rooms] room(s)?"
3. **Room Type & Features**:
   - "What room type is available, and what is the maximum occupancy?"
4. **Total Price & Taxes**:
   - "What is the total price for the full stay? Are all government taxes and service charges included in that quote?"
5. **Breakfast Details**:
   - "Is daily breakfast included for all guests, or is there a separate daily charge?"
6. **Cancellation & Deposit Terms**:
   - "What is your cancellation policy? Is there a free cancellation deadline, and is an advance deposit required?"
7. **Transport & Timing**:
   - "Do you provide airport transfer, and is early check-in or late checkout possible?"
