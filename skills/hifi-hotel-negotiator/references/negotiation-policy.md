# Hotel Rate Negotiation Policy & Etiquette

## Core Principles

1. **Polite, Non-Aggressive Inquiries**: Negotiation must remain courteous and professional at all times.
2. **Value-Add Priorities**:
   - Priority 1: Direct monetary discount on the total stay price.
   - Priority 2: Complimentary breakfast for all guests.
   - Priority 3: Complimentary airport pickup / drop.
   - Priority 4: Complimentary room category upgrade.
   - Priority 5: Guaranteed late checkout.
3. **Strict Constraints**:
   - If the receptionist declines any discounts or perks, gracefully accept the initial quoted rate without pushing repeatedly.
   - Never negotiate above the user-specified budget cap.
   - Always repeat the final confirmed terms at the end of the phone call to ensure clarity.
