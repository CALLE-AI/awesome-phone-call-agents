# Research Papers & Official Documentation

These sources provide adjacent research inspiration, not validation of this local word-list classifier or phone-call outcomes.

1. **Study of deep learning cues for cross linguistic part of speech tagging in English- Malayalam code-mixed data** (PubMed, 2026)
   - **URL/PMID**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42729234/
   - **Relevance**: Analyzes NLP architectures for processing and parsing highly code-mixed textual data.

2. **Preliminary Evidence for the Effects of Bilingual ECoLoGiC Treatment for People With Aphasia** (PubMed, 2026)
   - **URL/PMID**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42721409/
   - **Relevance**: Reports bilingual aphasia treatment; it is not a phone-agent rapport or code-switching validation study.

3. **ZSCAN-DDIE: an interpretable zero-shot learning method for biomedical text** (PubMed, 2026)
   - **URL/PMID**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42732332/
   - **Relevance**: Describes drug-drug-interaction text classification, not a validation of code-switching detection.

4. **ISO/IEC 42001 AI Management System** (ISO, 2023)
   - **URL**: https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html
   - **Relevance**: Framework for establishing inclusive, non-biased, and transparent AI systems, strictly avoiding stereotyping in language models.
