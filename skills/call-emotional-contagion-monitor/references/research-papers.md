# Research Scope — Emotional Contagion Monitor

This demonstration uses a small lexicon to flag patterns in supplied transcripts.
It does not measure an LLM's internal emotions or guarantee de-escalation.

Related background includes [EmotionBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03656)
and [Chain-of-Affect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12283). These papers are background
reading, not validation of this implementation, its thresholds, or its prompt
suggestions. No detection-rate or patch-success measurement is supplied.

The original regulatory summaries are not a compliance determination. This
offline tool does not apply prompts, alter a live call, or decide whether a
particular interaction violates a law or standard. Review suggestions manually.
