# Research References: Emotional Contagion Monitor

This skill relies on heavily verified 2024-2026 academic literature demonstrating the affective vulnerabilities of Large Language Models, as well as European regulatory standards for AI safety.

## Core Academic Papers

1. **Emotionally Numb or Empathetic? Evaluating How LLMs Feel Using Emotion Contagion**
   - **Identifier:** arXiv:2308.03656
   - **Link:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03656)
   - **Relevance:** Introduces the "EmotionBench" dataset. Proves that LLMs naturally align their sentiment to the input text, which is dangerous in a customer service context where mirroring anger escalates the situation.

2. **Large Language Models have Chain-of-Affective LLMs-CoA**
   - **Identifier:** arXiv:2512.12283
   - **Link:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12283)
   - **Relevance:** Highlights how sustained negative interactions cause LLMs to experience "affective overload" or "defensive numbing," degrading their professional output quality and causing them to break character.

3. **How Affect Propagates among LLM Agents: Emergent Emotional Contagion in Crowd Simulation**
   - **Identifier:** arXiv:2607.25140
   - **Link:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25140](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25140)
   - **Relevance:** Simulates the perception-appraisal-expression loop, demonstrating how easily alarm and hostility can spread when LLMs fail to apply emotional firewalls.

## Official Regulatory Documentation

1. **ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Information technology — Artificial intelligence**
   - **Link:** [https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)
   - **Relevance:** Mandates risk controls for "Hostile/Harmful Output Generation." An agent that mirrors customer aggression violates these safety guardrails.

2. **EU AI Act (Art. 5 - Prohibited AI Practices)**
   - **Link:** [https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/)
   - **Relevance:** Prohibits AI from deploying techniques that materially distort a person's behavior. An AI that escalates a user's emotional distress could fall under manipulative practices.
